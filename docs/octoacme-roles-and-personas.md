# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Key Interactions
- **Product Managers**: Receive acceptance criteria and prioritization
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate schedules and dependencies
- **UX Designers**: Collaborate on implementation of designs
- **Business Analysts**: Clarify requirements and acceptance criteria
- **QA/Testing**: Support test planning and validation
- **Security Champions**: Address security review feedback

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics
- Align with Customer Support and UX on market feedback

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Key Interactions
- **Project Managers**: Weekly alignment on schedules and scope
- **Developers**: Discuss technical feasibility and trade-offs
- **UX Designers**: Define user experience and flows
- **Business Analysts**: Clarify business requirements
- **Customer Support**: Gather user feedback and pain points
- **Scrum Masters**: Collaborate on agile ceremonies and iteration planning

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication
- Escalate blockers and dependencies (Level 1→2→3)

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Key Interactions
- **Product Managers**: Align on priorities, scope, and releases
- **Developers**: Track progress, manage blockers, plan sprints
- **Scrum Masters**: Coordinate agile practices and ceremonies
- **Business Analysts**: Clarify scope and requirements
- **Security Champions**: Track security risks and remediation
- **All Stakeholders**: Regular status updates and escalations

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation
- Blocker escalation chains

---

## Scrum Master

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove impediments to team progress, and foster continuous improvement. They act as servant-leaders, helping the team self-organize and maintain sustainable pace.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help resolve team blockers and impediments
- Mentor team members on agile practices and ceremonies
- Maintain sprint board and tracking tools
- Coach Product Managers on backlog refinement and story definition
- Nurture psychological safety and blameless retrospectives

### Goals
- Enable team self-organization and high velocity
- Foster continuous improvement and learning culture
- Reduce process friction and ceremony overhead
- Build team cohesion and psychological safety

### Key Interactions
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate on escalations and timeline management
- **Developers**: Support day-to-day impediment removal
- **Product Managers**: Guide backlog prioritization and refinement
- **All Team Members**: Facilitate ceremonies and retrospectives

### Typical Communication
- Facilitation of agile ceremonies
- Notes and action items from retrospectives
- One-on-one coaching and mentoring
- Agile metrics and velocity tracking

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers create user-centered designs, prototypes, and experiences that ensure products are intuitive, accessible, and delightful. They advocate for user needs and usability throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Design user flows, wireframes, prototypes, and interfaces
- Create design systems and UI guidelines
- Collaborate with developers on implementation and responsiveness
- Gather feedback from Customer Support and users
- Ensure accessibility standards are met

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, user-focused experiences
- Reduce support burden through better UX
- Validate solutions with users early and often
- Maintain design consistency and quality

### Key Interactions
- **Product Managers**: Understand vision, requirements, and priorities
- **Developers**: Discuss technical feasibility and implementation details
- **Customer Support**: Gather user pain points and feedback
- **Business Analysts**: Clarify business rules and edge cases
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate timeline and design review gates

### Typical Communication
- Design specs and component documentation
- Prototype reviews and user testing results
- Design critiques and feedback sessions
- Design-dev handoff meetings

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge business requirements and technical implementation. They elicit, document, and clarify requirements; map business processes; and ensure clear understanding across all stakeholders.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct stakeholder interviews and requirements elicitation
- Document business rules, workflows, and acceptance criteria
- Create process flows and requirement specifications
- Validate requirements with stakeholders and product team
- Identify scope gaps and dependencies
- Support UAT planning and execution

### Goals
- Prevent scope ambiguity and rework
- Ensure requirements are clear, complete, and testable
- Reduce misalignment and miscommunication
- Support project success through clarity

### Key Interactions
- **Product Managers**: Clarify vision and prioritization
- **Developers**: Validate technical feasibility and edge cases
- **Project Managers**: Identify dependencies and scope risks
- **UX Designers**: Map business logic to user flows
- **Stakeholders**: Elicit and validate requirements
- **QA/Testing**: Define test scenarios from requirements

### Typical Communication
- Requirements specifications and documentation
- Process flow diagrams and business rule matrices
- Requirements workshops and meetings
- Acceptance criteria refinement sessions

---

## Customer Support Representative

### Role Summary
Customer Support Representatives are the voice of the customer within the project team. They gather user feedback, track issues and pain points, and ensure the product delivers real customer value. They inform prioritization and design decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Document customer feedback, feature requests, and issues
- Identify patterns in customer pain points
- Support user acceptance testing and feedback collection
- Communicate customer impact of defects and delays
- Train users on new features and improvements
- Escalate critical customer issues to project team

### Goals
- Ensure product meets real customer needs
- Reduce support burden through better product design
- Provide early warning of product or usability issues
- Maintain customer satisfaction and retention

### Key Interactions
- **Product Managers**: Provide market feedback for prioritization
- **UX Designers**: Report usability issues and improvement suggestions
- **Developers**: Escalate critical bugs and customer blockers
- **Project Managers**: Communicate customer impact of delays
- **Business Analysts**: Clarify customer use cases and workflows

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback summaries and trend reports
- Feature request and bug reports with customer impact
- Participation in design reviews and UAT
- Customer webinars and training materials

---

## Security Champion

### Role Summary
Security Champions advocate for security best practices, design, and architecture across the project. They perform security reviews, escalate risks, and ensure compliance with security standards.

### Responsibilities
- Participate in architecture and design reviews from a security perspective
- Review code changes and configurations for security issues
- Maintain security threat models and risk assessments
- Escalate security vulnerabilities and ensure timely remediation
- Ensure compliance with security policies and standards
- Conduct security training and awareness sessions

### Goals
- Prevent security vulnerabilities and data breaches
- Build security into the development process
- Maintain customer trust and regulatory compliance
- Reduce security-related incidents and post-release issues

### Key Interactions
- **Developers**: Review code and provide security feedback
- **Project Managers**: Track security risks and remediation timelines
- **UX Designers**: Ensure secure authentication and data handling UX
- **Business Analysts**: Identify security requirements and edge cases
- **Architects**: Collaborate on secure system design

### Typical Communication
- Security review comments and findings
- Threat model documentation
- Security incident escalations
- Security training and awareness materials
